# Finance evidence policy

Evidence tiers:

- `S0`: exchange or issuer disclosure.
- `S1`: regulator or central-bank publication.
- `S4`: media, market commentary, or social discovery signal.

S4 can trigger investigation but cannot replace S0/S1 facts. Briefings must separate fact, inference, and unknowns. They must not generate buy, sell, target-price, position-size, stop-loss, take-profit, or return promises.
